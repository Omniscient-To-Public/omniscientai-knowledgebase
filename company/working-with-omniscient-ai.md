# Working With Omniscient AI

## How does an engagement begin?

An engagement begins by identifying the business process, users, current systems, constraints, and desired
outcomes. Omniscient AI can help map the current workflow and identify where custom software, integration,
automation, or AI may provide practical value.

## What information is useful for an initial conversation?

Useful non-sensitive information includes:

- The workflow or customer journey that needs improvement
- The teams and users involved
- Current applications, databases, or communication channels
- Repetitive steps, delays, handoff problems, or common exceptions
- Required approvals, permissions, or escalation rules
- The business measures that would indicate improvement

Sensitive credentials, private records, and confidential customer data should not be submitted through the
public website voice demo.

## Does Omniscient AI offer fixed pricing?

The public website does not publish fixed prices. Scope and commercial terms depend on the selected
capabilities, integrations, delivery requirements, and operating constraints.

For pricing or a project discussion, use the Omniscient AI contact page at `/contact-us`.

## Does Omniscient AI guarantee results or timelines?

No public guarantee or standard delivery timeline is stated. Expected outcomes and schedules need to be
evaluated for the specific project, available systems, data readiness, integration complexity, and review
requirements.
