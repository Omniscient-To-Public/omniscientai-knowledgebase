# Nexus by Atlas Contact Center

## What is Nexus by Atlas?

Nexus by Atlas is Omniscient AI's contact center for human agents and supervisors. Agents take calls in a
browser phone, supervisors monitor live calls, and administrators manage users, teams, queues, routing, and
business hours from one web workspace.

Nexus by Atlas works alongside the Atlas AI Platform, so a call or conversation that needs a person arrives with
what Atlas already learned. An organization can also use Nexus for its own team's calls.

Relevant public page: `/atlas/nexus`.

## How does Nexus by Atlas work with Atlas?

When Atlas hands a customer to a person, Nexus by Atlas receives the reason, a summary, the caller's name, the
intent, key facts, and the recent AI conversation, and shows them to the agent who takes the call.

On a warm transfer, Atlas briefs the agent before the caller is connected: Atlas explains who is calling and
why, answers the agent's questions, and asks whether to put the caller through. The caller hears hold music
while this happens.

Atlas can also hand over WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, and TikTok conversations. Agents claim a
conversation, reply, and then hand it back to Atlas or close it. The channel's usual reply window applies.

For urgent reports, Atlas can queue an urgent callback in Nexus. Atlas briefs the agent who picks it
up before the customer is called back.

## How does Nexus by Atlas route calls?

Nexus by Atlas queues calls by priority and rings an available agent in the queue's teams. Unanswered calls
return to the queue.

Each Nexus queue can have business hours, public holidays, a maximum wait, and a fallback such as
voicemail, another number, an overflow queue, or a callback. Calls handed over by Atlas can be routed by
topic to the right queue.

## What can Nexus by Atlas agents do on a call?

Nexus by Atlas agents can answer, decline, mute, hold, end, and transfer calls. They can consult a colleague
before handing a call over (warm transfer), call customers from the desk, and take queued callbacks.

During a call, the Nexus Live Analysis panel shows a live transcript, the call topic, key facts,
approved answer hints, and warnings such as caller frustration, a possible emergency, or a sensitive topic.
These are AI suggestions for the agent to review, not assessments.

## What happens after a Nexus by Atlas call?

After a Nexus call ends, the agent sees an AI summary and can tidy up notes with AI, set a
disposition, and create a follow-up ticket, including a callback reminder. Nexus keeps a caller profile
that remembers past calls, summaries, preferences, and open issues for that caller.

## What can Nexus by Atlas supervisors do?

Nexus by Atlas supervisors see every live call on a Supervisor Desk, with caller mood and warnings that need
attention shown first. A supervisor can listen live, whisper to the agent only, or join the call so everyone
hears them.

## Does Nexus by Atlas have quality scoring and reports?

Yes. Nexus by Atlas can score answered calls against an organization's own QA scorecards, with a reason for
each criterion. Supervisors can review and override scores.

Nexus reports cover service level, average wait, abandonment, talk time, queue and agent performance,
outbound calls, dispositions, and topics. Standard reports can be downloaded as CSV.

## What are the limits of Nexus by Atlas?

Nexus by Atlas does not run outbound calling campaigns, connect SIP desk phones, or forecast staffing. Its
reports are not a workforce-management or billing system.

Nexus live transcription supports English, and Mandarin where an organization has it set up. Caller mood
scoring works in English.

WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, and TikTok conversations reach Nexus only as handoffs from
Atlas.
