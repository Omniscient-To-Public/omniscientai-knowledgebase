# Atlas Nexus Contact Center

## What is Atlas Nexus?

Atlas Nexus is Omniscient AI's contact center for human agents and supervisors. Agents take calls in a
browser phone, supervisors monitor live calls, and administrators manage users, teams, queues, routing, and
business hours from one web workspace.

Atlas Nexus works alongside the Atlas AI Platform, so a call or conversation that needs a person arrives with
what Atlas already learned. An organization can also use Atlas Nexus for its own team's calls.

Relevant public page: `/atlas/nexus`.

## How does Atlas Nexus work with Atlas?

When Atlas hands a customer to a person, Atlas Nexus receives the reason, a summary, the caller's name, the
intent, key facts, and the recent AI conversation, and shows them to the agent who takes the call.

On a warm transfer, Atlas briefs the agent before the caller is connected: Atlas explains who is calling and
why, answers the agent's questions, and asks whether to put the caller through. The caller hears hold music
while this happens.

Atlas can also hand over WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, and TikTok conversations. Agents claim a
conversation, reply, and then hand it back to Atlas or close it. The channel's usual reply window applies.

For urgent reports, Atlas can queue an urgent callback in Atlas Nexus. Atlas briefs the agent who picks it
up before the customer is called back.

## How does Atlas Nexus route calls?

Atlas Nexus queues calls by priority and arrival time and offers each call to the available agent in the
queue's teams who has been idle longest. Unanswered calls return to the queue.

Each Atlas Nexus queue can have business hours, public holidays, a maximum wait, and a fallback such as
voicemail, another number, an overflow queue, or a callback. Calls handed over by Atlas can be routed by
topic to the right queue.

## What can Atlas Nexus agents do on a call?

Atlas Nexus agents can answer, decline, mute, hold, end, and transfer calls. They can consult a colleague
before handing a call over (warm transfer), call customers from the desk, and take queued callbacks.

During a call, the Atlas Nexus Live Analysis panel shows a live transcript, the call topic, key facts,
approved answer hints, and warnings such as caller frustration, a possible emergency, or a sensitive topic.
These are AI suggestions for the agent to review, not assessments.

## What happens after an Atlas Nexus call?

After an Atlas Nexus call ends, the agent sees an AI summary and can tidy up notes with AI, set a
disposition, and create a follow-up ticket, including a callback reminder. Atlas Nexus keeps a caller profile
that remembers past calls, summaries, preferences, and open issues for that caller.

## What can Atlas Nexus supervisors do?

Atlas Nexus supervisors see every live call on a Supervisor Desk, with caller mood and warnings that need
attention shown first. A supervisor can listen live, whisper to the agent only, or join the call so everyone
hears them.

## Does Atlas Nexus have quality scoring and reports?

Yes. Atlas Nexus can score answered calls against an organization's own QA scorecards, with a reason for
each criterion. Supervisors can review and override scores.

Atlas Nexus reports cover service level, average wait, abandonment, talk time, queue and agent performance,
outbound calls, dispositions, and topics. Standard reports can be downloaded as CSV.

## What are the limits of Atlas Nexus?

Atlas Nexus does not run outbound calling campaigns, connect SIP desk phones, or forecast staffing. Its
reports are not a workforce-management or billing system.

Atlas Nexus live transcription supports English, or English and Mandarin in one call depending on the
organization's configuration. Malay is not transcribed. Caller mood scoring works in English.

WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, and TikTok conversations reach Atlas Nexus only as handoffs from
Atlas.
