# Software Development

## What software development services does Omniscient AI provide?

Omniscient AI designs and builds enterprise systems, internal tools, customer-facing applications,
backend services, APIs, databases, and cloud-native products for real business operations.

## What types of systems can be built?

Examples include:

- Enterprise platforms for workflows, approvals, and reporting
- Customer portals
- Internal business applications
- Responsive web and mobile-ready applications
- APIs, backend services, databases, and job-processing systems
- Data dashboards and decision-support tools
- Modern replacements for fragmented or legacy tools

## What parts of delivery are supported?

Support can cover discovery, requirements, architecture, product and interface design, implementation,
integration, quality assurance, deployment, monitoring, documentation, and ongoing improvement.

## How are maintainability and scale addressed?

Software can be designed with clear architecture, role-based access, API-first integration, automated
testing, deployment pipelines, observability, and practical documentation.

The appropriate architecture depends on expected usage, data volume, security needs, availability
requirements, and the organization's existing technology.

## Does Omniscient AI only build AI products?

No. Omniscient AI provides broader software consultancy and engineering. AI may be part of the solution
when useful, but conventional software, integration, and workflow design remain important parts of
reliable systems.
