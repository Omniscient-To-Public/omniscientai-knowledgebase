# AI Business Automation

## What is AI business automation?

AI business automation uses connected workflows and AI-assisted steps to reduce repetitive operational
work. Omniscient AI designs automations around existing tools and keeps people involved where judgment,
approval, or exception handling matters.

## What can be included?

An AI business automation engagement may include:

- Process mapping and identification of automation opportunities
- Workflow triggers, approvals, updates, and notifications
- Classification and routing of requests
- Document and data extraction, summarization, validation, and movement
- Human review checkpoints and escalation paths
- Dashboards for cycle time, throughput, exceptions, and workflow health
- Integration with CRM, help-desk, database, cloud, and internal systems

## What are common use cases?

Common use cases include:

- Customer-support triage, summaries, routing, and follow-up
- Sales lead enrichment and CRM updates
- Finance and administrative approvals or reminders
- Employee onboarding and internal request coordination
- Operational exception monitoring and owner notification

## How is reliability addressed?

Reliable automation requires clear ownership, permissions, auditability, monitoring, fallback paths, and
exception handling. Automation should not hide failures or remove human control from sensitive decisions.

## When is AI business automation a good fit?

It is a good fit when a team repeatedly performs the same structured steps across many requests, records,
customers, or systems. Discovery is needed when the process is inconsistent, lacks an authoritative data
source, or depends heavily on undocumented judgment.
