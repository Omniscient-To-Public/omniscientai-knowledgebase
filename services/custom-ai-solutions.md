# Custom AI Solutions

## What are custom AI solutions?

Custom AI solutions are systems designed around a specific organization's workflows, data, users, and
operational constraints. They are useful when a generic AI tool does not fit the required process,
integration, governance, or customer experience.

## What can Omniscient AI build?

Possible custom AI work includes:

- Customer-service and voice agents
- Internal knowledge assistants
- Workflow and operations automation
- Decision-support tools
- Document-processing systems
- Lead qualification and sales support
- Analytics, recommendations, and forecasting workflows
- AI-enabled features inside existing products or internal applications

## What is included in an engagement?

Depending on scope, an engagement may include workflow discovery, agent or model design, system
integration, application development, prototype-to-production delivery, human review controls, testing,
deployment, and performance monitoring.

## How are custom AI systems connected to a business?

They can connect to approved CRMs, support platforms, databases, cloud services, documents, APIs, and
internal applications. Access should be limited by role, workflow, and business need.

## How is quality managed?

Quality can be managed through approved knowledge sources, evaluation cases, validation, human review,
monitoring for accuracy and latency, cost tracking, audit trails, and iterative improvement based on real
usage.

No particular accuracy, savings, or business result is guaranteed without a defined use case and measured
implementation.
